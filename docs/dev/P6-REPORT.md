## The P5 carry-over fix (F1, S3)

`admin/moderation.decide` previously left P2's automatic freezes in place on dismiss/warn while SCR-A03 promises the moderator chooses, and dismiss's NTF-14 said "no action taken" even when the account stayed frozen. Now:

- `decide` accepts `freezeChoice: "lift" | "keep"`, **required** (`ERR_VALIDATION`, field `freezeChoice`) exactly when a dismiss/warn targets an item whose auto-freezes are still in place — account freeze (linked report `autoAction === "account_frozen"` ∧ subject still `frozen`) and/or request reveals freeze (`autoAction === "reveals_frozen"` ∧ request still `revealsFrozen`). Restore already lifted both; suspend/ban supersede them; hide_request never interacted.
- Lift patches the subject back to `active` (+ donor `live` recompute) and/or clears `revealsFrozen`. The audit row records `freezeChoice` + `freezesLifted[]`; the `moderation_decided` event gains `freeze: lifted|kept|none`.
- **NTF-14 wording is built from the real outcome** by the pure, unit-tested `decisionActionText` in `convex/rules/moderation.ts`: dismiss+kept = "Report reviewed — no further action taken. The safety hold from this report stays in place."; dismiss+lifted = "…the safety hold from this report has been lifted."; warn mirrors it; the plain pre-P6 texts are unchanged when no freeze exists. "No action taken" can no longer be sent while a freeze stands.
- The SCR-A03 client shows the lift/keep choice when a freeze is in place and disables dismiss/warn until chosen. Tests: 8 integration cases (choice-required, keep, lift incl. donor-live restoration, warn both ways, reveals-freeze both ways, both freezes on one item, no-freeze back-compat, already-lifted-elsewhere) + 5 wording-matrix unit tests.

## Interpretation notes

1. **Freeze detection is report-derived.** There is no freeze flag on moderation items; an auto-freeze is "still in place" iff a linked report carries the P2 auto-action AND the frozen state still holds on the subject/request. If another decision already lifted it, no choice is demanded — matching SCR-A03's intent (the choice exists to decide the freeze's fate, not as ceremony).
2. **Re-consent compares against the LIVE settings version**, not the bundled fallback, whenever the backend answers — the fallback exists so a first-run/offline client still gates on *something*; the server-side `needsConsent` (profile version vs settings) is the backstop that cannot be bypassed by a stale client.
3. **Landing routes differ by platform deliberately**: specs SCR-P01 is the public *web* front door; on native, `/` keeps the P1 redirect behaviour (signed-in → home, else welcome) so the installed app never lands on a marketing page.
4. **About-page team naming follows vision.md §8 exactly** — the one named person is the founder; the other four members appear by role. Adding their names is a team decision for the P7 polish pass, not something to invent here.
5. **The bundle job failing is the honest state.** Marking X03 "done" would require either splitting the bundle (a bundling-architecture change with regression risk across all 52 screens — wrong thing to rush in the hardening phase) or quietly raising the budget (specs are the source of truth; not ours to edit). P6 measures it, fixes the one safe win (fonts), wires the gate, and hands P7 a precise target: **−547,018 B gzip**.
