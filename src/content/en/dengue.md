## Dengue & platelets

This page is a **draft** prepared for the UDVASITO pilot. It restates the same general guidance as the app's FAQ and has not yet had its medical review. The hospital and blood bank decide what a patient receives.

## Why platelets?

Platelets are the part of blood that helps it clot. Many severe dengue patients need platelets rather than whole blood, because dengue can lower the platelet count. When a family posts a request and chooses the **Dengue patient** option, the request automatically asks for platelets.

## How a dengue request works here

- The request goes only to donors who **opted in to platelet donation** and match the patient's **exact blood group**.
- Platelet donors can generally donate again sooner than whole-blood donors — the app applies a **14-day** wait after a platelet donation and shows your next eligible date.
- If you recently recovered from dengue yourself, wait until you are fully recovered and the blood bank accepts you — they make the final decision on the day.
