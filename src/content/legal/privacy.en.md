## Draft notice

This is a **draft** privacy notice for the UDVASITO pilot. It has not yet had its final legal review. It describes honestly what the pilot system does with your information today.

## Who we are

UDVASITO is a free, student-run blood-donor network from the University of Chittagong. We are not a blood bank, hospital or medical service, and we never charge money.

## What we collect

- Your name, mobile number and date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or older).
- If you register as a donor: your blood group, gender (used only to calculate donation intervals), area (division, district, upazila/thana), last donation date and type, and whether you can donate platelets.
- If you post a request: the blood group and component needed, the hospital and area, when it is needed, and a contact number for the patient party.
- Records of matches: who was notified, who accepted, and when a phone number was shown to another member.
- Basic technical logs needed to keep the service safe and working.

## Why we use it

- To match blood requests with nearby donors who can donate.
- To show a phone number to the other side **only after a donor confirms** they can help — and only according to the sharing choice that donor made.
- To enforce safety rules (donation intervals, request limits, abuse prevention).

## Phone numbers

- Your number is never public and never shown on any listing.
- A number is revealed only inside a confirmed match, or to show you an on-duty volunteer's number when no donor has responded. Every reveal is recorded in a private log that admins can audit.
- If a donor chooses "I'll call them myself", the requester never sees the donor's number.

## Health information

Donor health-related details (blood group, donation dates) are stored only with your separate consent, which you give during donor setup. You can withdraw by deleting your donor profile or your account.

## Phone verification

We verify numbers through Telegram (you share your own contact with our bot) or in person with a volunteer or admin. We do not buy or sell numbers, and we never send marketing messages.

## AI features

Some optional features (for example, reading a pasted blood-request post) use an AI service. Before anything is sent, names, phone numbers, email addresses and medical details are removed by our code. Matching and eligibility decisions are never made by AI.

## Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only as described above — between matched members, and with the services that run UDVASITO (hosting and database providers) under their own privacy terms.

## Keeping and deleting your data

You can download your data or delete your account from Settings. Deletion removes your personal information; anonymised statistics may remain. Safety and audit logs are kept only as long as needed to prevent abuse.

## Children

UDVASITO is for people aged 18 and over. Accounts found to belong to younger people are blocked.

## Changes and contact

If this notice changes in an important way, we will ask for your consent again in the app. Questions: contact the UDVASITO team through the app's help options.
