## Draft notice

These are **draft** terms for the UDVASITO pilot, pending final legal review (DEP-02). By continuing in the app you accept them for the pilot.

## What UDVASITO is — and is not

- UDVASITO connects people who need blood with volunteer donors nearby. It is a free community service run by students.
- We are **not** a blood bank, hospital or medical service. We do not collect, test, store or transport blood.
- Blood donation and transfusion decisions always belong to hospitals and their medical staff.

## No money, ever

- UDVASITO is completely free. Nobody may ask for money, gifts or favours for blood through this service.
- If anyone asks you for money, do not pay — report it in the app.

## Your responsibilities

- You must be 18 or older to use UDVASITO.
- Give true information: your name, your number, your blood group and donation dates.
- Donors: only accept a request if you are genuinely able and well enough to donate. The hospital will screen you before donation; UDVASITO's checks do not replace that screening.
- Requesters: post only real needs, and close your request when it is fulfilled or no longer needed.
- Do not misuse anyone's phone number. Numbers are shared for one purpose only: arranging that donation.

## Safety

- Meet at the hospital or blood bank. UDVASITO does not arrange meetings elsewhere.
- We may pause or close accounts that break these terms, post fake requests, ask for money, or harass members.

## Availability and liability

- The service is provided as-is for the pilot. We work hard to keep it running and accurate, but we cannot guarantee a donor will always be found.
- In an emergency, always contact the hospital and emergency services directly — do not rely only on this app.

## Changes

If these terms change in an important way, we will show the new version in the app and ask you to accept it again.
